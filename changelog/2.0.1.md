## Important

- The Raycast extension no longer has a Menu Bar command, since Pond already shows its own menu bar icon.

## New

- Shop and book links save as a Product, with the product image as the cover.
- Meet Your Fish introduces your fish once after you update, and shows that your earlier chats now belong to the default fish.
- Your fish turn in 3D, and the Fish section has a gallery and clearer status.
- Pond shows at most one prompt per launch, like a save milestone or MCP setup for Cursor and Claude Desktop, and never over another dialog.
- The browser extension dialog includes Safari and opens Safari at Pond's extension settings, or tells you why it can't.
- Pond tells you when refreshing a save needs you to sign in to its source.

## Improved

- Pond pauses fish motion, GIF playback, and enrichment while it's idle or your Mac is warm, and stops on-device inference when your Mac is hot.
- Sensitive content detection uses a new on-device model, and the blur stays inside the card.
- Text-only posts no longer get a sensitive content score, and Pond clears the ones they had.
- Blur Sensitive Thumbnails stays visible and dimmed while detection is off.
- A divider replaces the library grid fade and shows only while saves scroll under the toolbar.
- Settings descriptions fit on one line, with extra detail in an info tooltip where it changes what you do.
- The detail pane no longer has a Style section.

## Fixed

- Library details are back, so you can tag saves from the grid again.
- Tags in the browser extension's save overlay can be selected and created.
- Cmd+F opens Search Current View.
- App shortcuts no longer fire while you type in a text field.
- Refresh from Source works on imported saves and updates their covers.
- Library processing finishes when a local caption model spends its answer on reasoning.
- Suggested tags work with large tag lists, and a tag list that fails no longer retries on every save.
- Pinterest syncs pins that still exist, including ones only visible when you're signed in.
- mymind imports skip binary fields that were read as tags.
- Pond skips broken site icons and repairs the ones already saved.
- The media count is readable in dark mode.
- Savee shows its real logo.
- Each fish mark floats and blinks on its own instead of in step with its neighbors.
