## Fixed

- The fish model picker lists models from OpenCode Go, OpenRouter, Azure, and Baseten.
- Dock Visibility set to Automatic hides the Dock icon when you close the window, and Always Visible keeps it.
- Save Page in the browser extension saves in one click instead of opening the panel.
- The check on the browser extension's toolbar icon is white.
- Likes and Bookmarks stay off after you leave an Account, and likes the extension sees aren't saved while Likes is off.
- A reply's screenshot shows the reply itself instead of the post it replies to.
- Pause and Cancel stop an X or Threads capture right away.
