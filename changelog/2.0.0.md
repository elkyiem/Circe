## New

- Tumblr syncs your likes with the time you liked each post.
- Savee syncs the items you saved, newest first.
- Behance syncs your appreciated projects with the date you appreciated them.
- Vimeo syncs your likes and your Watch Later list.
- Dribbble syncs the shots you liked.
- Unsplash syncs your bookmarked photos.
- Bluesky syncs your public likes after you enter your handle, and your bookmarks once you sign in to bsky.app from the Account.
- GitHub syncs the repositories you star and keeps each README to read in Pond.
- Substack syncs your saved and liked posts, and checks the publications you subscribe to for new posts every six hours.
- Import Data Export reads your X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and Reddit archives and adds every like and save, with its original date when the archive has one.
- YouTube playlists from Google Takeout become folders in your library.
- Fish can run on Apple Intelligence, the model built into macOS 26, with no key or host to run.
- Your fish can call tools from MCP servers you run, and anything that could change something waits for your review.
- Share to Pond from any Mac app's Share menu to save links, images, and files.
- Shortcuts can save a link with `pond://save`, adding tags and a folder in the background.
- The Alfred workflow searches your library with `pond` and saves a link with `psave`.
